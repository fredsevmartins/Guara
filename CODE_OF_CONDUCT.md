# Code of Conduct

Guara welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or scientific position.
Contributors and maintainers should communicate respectfully and evaluate technical claims with evidence.

## Expected conduct

- Give specific, constructive feedback and distinguish disagreement from personal criticism.
- Respect different levels of experience and correct mistakes without humiliation.
- Credit others' contributions and respect privacy and confidential research material.
- Follow the project's evidence and synthetic-data guidance when reporting concerns.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct, targeted personal attacks,
and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Persistent disruption
or retaliation against good-faith reports is also unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official project spaces.
Report concerns privately to the repository owner through available GitHub profile contact options.
Do not post allegations, personal information, or evidence containing private material in public
issues. No dedicated reporting address or response-time guarantee is claimed. For a software
vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or exclude
contributors according to severity and recurrence. When appropriate, explain the relevant conduct
and corrective action while protecting reporter privacy. Reports involving a maintainer should be
handled without requiring public confrontation. Decisions may be questioned privately through the
same contact route; the project does not claim an independent appeal panel or guaranteed outcome.
